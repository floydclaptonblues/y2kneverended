OPERATING RULES

Built to help, not harvest.

“Free” means the completed work is not held hostage. “AI-assisted” means the model’s role is named precisely rather than used as decoration.

No false doors

No fake download buttons, forced registrations, hidden subscriptions, or artificial countdowns.

Local when possible

Tools process files on the user’s device whenever browser technology can do the job safely.

Ordinary formats

Users can export work without being trapped inside a proprietary account or project file.

Errors stay visible

Failed approaches and missing variables are preserved when they help the next person.

Humans remain accountable

Consequential decisions are not disguised as automatic certainty.

No miracle language

Claims are limited to what the code, evidence, and documented operation actually establish.

Development status: this is the first public foundation. Working tools are labeled “Live.” Planned work is labeled “Development.” The custom `.net` domain will be connected after the site is broad enough to represent its full public-service mission.
